How AI Bridge works ⓘ

Choose a plan for what you want to do in Warframe. Add a specific item, goal, amount, or time limit so the advice fits your session. The description below the menu explains each plan.

Include chooses what account and live game information to attach. Recommended keeps useful context readable; Compact shortens the data; Raw includes more detail for players who want to inspect it.

Copy prompt + profile is the usual choice: paste it into an AI assistant of your choice. Copy prompt omits account data. Copy profile shares only the selected data. Preview before copying, especially if you plan to share it.

Tenno Link prepares text locally and does not contact an AI service. The receiving assistant should check current patch notes and sources, but its answer can still be wrong. Confirm builds, drops, and mission access in game.

Your details stay in this open window until you copy them.

EXPORT MODE

INCLUDE
